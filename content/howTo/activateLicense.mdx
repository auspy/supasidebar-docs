# How to activate a license

Add your license key from inside SupaSidebar to unlock Pro on this Mac. The whole flow lives in **Preferences → About → License Status**.

<Callout type="info">
Your license key is in your purchase email. Copy-paste it, don't retype it - keys are case-sensitive and extra whitespace causes activation to fail.
</Callout>

## Steps

### Step 1: Open Settings

Open the sidebar and click the gear icon at the top to open **Preferences**.

![SupaSidebar sidebar with the Settings gear icon highlighted](/assets/activate-license-settings-gear.png)

### Step 2: Go to About and view your license

In the Preferences sidebar, select **About**. Scroll to the **Actions** section and click **View License** next to **License Status**.

![About preferences panel showing License Status with the View License button](/assets/activate-license-about-panel.png)

### Step 3: Enter your license key

The **Activate SupaSidebar** window opens. Paste your license key into the **Enter your license key** field, then click **Activate**.

![Activate SupaSidebar window with the license key field and Activate button](/assets/activate-license-window.png)

Once the key is accepted, **Current Status** shows **Licensed** and Pro features unlock right away. You can close the window.

## Managing devices

Each license activates on a limited number of Macs. Device management, including removing an old Mac to free a slot, happens on the **Manage License** page on the web, not in the app.

<Callout type="info">
There is no in-app deactivate button. To free a device slot, sign in on the Manage License page at [supasidebar.com](https://supasidebar.com) with your purchase email and remove the device you no longer use.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Invalid license key" | Copy-paste the key from your purchase email - don't retype it. Keys are case-sensitive. |
| Activation fails, device limit reached | Remove an old Mac from the Manage License page, then activate again. |
| Don't have a key yet | Start a free trial from the same window, or buy a license at [supasidebar.com/pricing](https://supasidebar.com/pricing). |
| Bought through BundleHunt | Your key is in your BundleHunt order email, not from SupaSidebar. |
