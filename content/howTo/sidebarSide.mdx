# Keep the Sidebar on the Left or Right

Pin SupaSidebar to one side of the screen so it always appears where you expect. This takes two steps: set the default position, then make sure your mouse trigger edges match.

Both steps matter. The default position decides which side the sidebar opens on, and the mouse trigger zones decide which screen edge summons it. If they disagree, the sidebar can feel like it appears on the "wrong" side.

## Step 1: Set the default side

1. Open **Preferences → Sidebar**.
2. Find **Default Sidebar Position** at the top of the Sidebar section.
3. Choose **Left** or **Right**.

![Default Sidebar Position set to Left or Right in Preferences → Sidebar](/assets/sidebar-default-position.png)

The sidebar now opens on that side every time, whether you open it with a shortcut, the floating button, or a mouse trigger.

> You can flip sides at any time with `Cmd Ctrl L`. This toggles the same Default Sidebar Position setting.

## Step 2: Match your mouse trigger edges

If you use [Mouse Triggers](/features/sidebarAccess/mouseTriggers) to reveal the sidebar by moving your cursor to a screen edge, turn off the edge you do not want. Otherwise moving your mouse to the opposite edge will still pull the sidebar to that side.

1. Open **Preferences → Mouse Triggers**.
2. In **Active Zones**, check only the edge that matches your default position:
   - Sidebar on the left → enable **Left Edge**, uncheck **Right Edge**.
   - Sidebar on the right → enable **Right Edge**, uncheck **Left Edge**.

![Active Zones showing Left Edge and Right Edge checkboxes in Preferences → Mouse Triggers](/assets/mouse-triggers-active-zones.png)

Leave both edges checked only if you want the sidebar to appear on whichever side your cursor reaches. With a single edge selected, the sidebar stays on your chosen side.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Sidebar still opens on the wrong side | Recheck **Default Sidebar Position** in **Preferences → Sidebar**. It controls the open side for every trigger. |
| Sidebar appears on the opposite edge with the mouse | Uncheck the unwanted edge under **Preferences → Mouse Triggers → Active Zones**. |
| Position keeps flipping unexpectedly | You may be pressing `Cmd Ctrl L`. That shortcut toggles left and right. |
| Mouse triggers do nothing | Grant [Accessibility permission](/gettingStarted/permissions#accessibility) and restart the app. |
