# Send feedback

Send an idea or report a problem directly from the app.

1. Open **Preferences → Feedback**.
2. Check **Your email** and edit it if you want replies sent to a different address.
3. Describe the idea or problem in **Your feedback**. For a bug, include what you did and what happened.
4. Click **Send** and wait for **Feedback sent**.

If sending fails, the form shows an error and keeps your message so you can retry. You do not need to retype it.
