# Claim Your Shipaton Discount

Building with us during Shipaton 2026? Here's how to redeem your Shipaton promo code at checkout.

## The Shipaton Offer

Two codes, each tied to a specific plan:

| Code | What you get | Plan it works on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SHIPATON26` | 2 months free (100% off, first 2 billing cycles) | **Monthly** only |
| `SHIPATON50` | 50% off | **Yearly** only |

Pick the plan that matches your code:
- Want 2 months free? Choose the **Monthly** plan and use `SHIPATON26`.
- Want 50% off? Choose the **Yearly** plan and use `SHIPATON50`.

If you apply a code to the wrong plan, it won't validate.

Offer runs the full Shipaton event: **August 1 – September 30, 2026.**

## How to Redeem

### Step 1: Open the Pricing Page

Go to [supasidebar.com/pricing](https://supasidebar.com/pricing).

### Step 2: Choose the Right Plan

- For `SHIPATON26` (2 months free), select **Monthly**.
- For `SHIPATON50` (50% off), select **Yearly**.

### Step 3: Enter Your Code

In the checkout overlay, find the **discount code** field and type or paste your code. Codes are **case-sensitive**  - enter them exactly as `SHIPATON26` or `SHIPATON50`.

### Step 4: Validate

Click **Validate**. Your updated price shows immediately.

### Step 5: Complete Your Purchase

Review the total and complete payment. Done.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Invalid code" error | Check for typos. Codes are case-sensitive and must be entered exactly. |
| "Code expired" error | The Shipaton offer ends September 30, 2026. |
| Code doesn't apply to your plan | `SHIPATON26` works only on the Monthly plan; `SHIPATON50` works only on the Yearly plan. Switch to the matching plan. |
| Discount not showing | Make sure you clicked **Validate** after entering the code. |

## Need Help?

If your code isn't working, reach out at [supasidebar.com/contact](https://supasidebar.com/contact) and we'll sort it out.
