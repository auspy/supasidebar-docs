# Fill Screen

Browser automatically resizes to fill the remaining screen space alongside the sidebar.

Choose **Maximize Browser Window** in the **When Sidebar Opens** menu to share the screen between your browser and the sidebar. The browser resizes to fill the space beside the sidebar, and the **When Sidebar Hides** menu controls what happens to the browser afterwards.

When the sidebar opens, your frontmost browser window resizes to fill the remaining space. When the sidebar hides, what happens to the browser depends on the **When Sidebar Hides** setting (see [Sidebar Panel - Behavior](/features/sidebar#behavior)).

## Setup

1. Open **Preferences → Sidebar**
2. In **Browser Integration**, set **When Sidebar Opens** to **Maximize Browser Window**
3. Choose what should happen under **When Sidebar Hides**. To expand the browser when the sidebar closes, select **Expand Browser to Fill Screen**
4. Grant [Accessibility permission](/gettingStarted/permissions#accessibility) when prompted

For a sidebar that stays at the screen edge while the browser resizes around it, see [Dock Mode](/features/sidebarAccess/dockmode).

## Works in both attached and independent layouts

**Maximize Browser Window** works with both **Smart Attach** and **Independent** sidebar integration. The **Full Screen Browser** button in the sidebar's top toolbar also fills the available space immediately, without changing your **When Sidebar Opens** setting.

## When Sidebar Hides

The **When Sidebar Hides** menu picks one of three behaviors once the sidebar goes away:

- **Expand Browser to Fill Screen** - the front browser expands to fill the screen.
- **Restore Browser Position** - browser windows return to the size and position they had before the sidebar opened.
- **Leave Browser As-Is** - the browser stays in its filled shape.

See [Sidebar Panel - Behavior](/features/sidebar#behavior) for the full picker.

## When to Use

- You want a split-screen layout between sidebar and browser
- You always use sidebar and browser together
- You prefer maximized use of screen space with no overlap
