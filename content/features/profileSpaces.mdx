# Link a browser or profile

Link a space to the browser or profile you use for that work. For example, link your Work space to **Chrome · Work**.

<Callout type="info">
These instructions apply to **SupaSidebar 1.3.0 and later**. [Using 1.2.x?](/versions/1.2/profileSpaces)
</Callout>

## What linking does

| Choice | Live Tabs in this space | Default destination for links |
|--------|-------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **No link** | Tabs from all enabled, supported browsers | Your existing link-opening settings |
| **A browser**, such as Chrome | Tabs from that browser | That browser |
| **A profile**, such as Chrome · Work | Tabs belonging to that profile | That profile |

A one-time **Open With** choice, a saved link's **Preferred Browser**, or a higher-priority Air Traffic Control rule can choose another destination. See [Browser Selection](/howTo/browserSelection#priority-order).

Bookmarks are optional. Linking does not automatically import bookmarks, sync changes back to the browser, or close tabs.

## Link a new or existing space

- **New space:** choose **Link a browser or profile** while creating the space. The ⓘ button explains the choice before you link it.
- **Existing space:** click the **link icon next to the space name**, then select a browser or profile.
- **From Preferences:** open **Preferences → Spaces → My spaces**, select the space, and manage its linked browser or profile.
- **From a space menu:** right-click its space icon or open its header options menu. Choose **Link a browser or profile…**, **Linked to…**, or **Space settings…** as appropriate.

The space header shows the browser and profile name, such as **Chrome · Work**. Click that label to check for bookmarks, review a permission issue, open **Space settings…**, or unlink.

The picker shows the available browser and profile choices. A browser may support linking the whole browser without supporting individual profiles. See [Browser Support](/gettingStarted/browserSupport).

In **SupaSidebar 1.3.1 and later**, an unlinked space in Preferences shows only the browser/profile picker. The Live Tabs, Opening links, and Bookmarks details appear after you link it.

## Add bookmarks when you want them

When creating a linked space, click **Fetch bookmarks**, review the results, and choose what to include. For an existing space, click its linked browser/profile label and choose **Check for new bookmarks in…**.

- **Named profile:** fetch bookmarks from that profile.
- **Whole browser:** for Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Firefox, and Zen, fetch bookmarks from all discovered profiles in that browser.
- **Safari:** fetch from Safari's bookmark library, including when a Safari profile is linked.
- **Unsupported source:** the connection menu explains that bookmark checking is unavailable.

Choose what to add. The browser's bookmarks stay unchanged. New bookmarks are checked when you request it; there is no background or two-way bookmark sync. If some profiles cannot be read, the warning names them and any readable results remain available to review.

The [Import Bookmarks guide](/howTo/browserImport) also covers importing a browser into separate spaces and importing an HTML file.

## Switching spaces

SupaSidebar can follow you to the right space when you move between browser windows. Click into a window from your Chrome · Work profile and the sidebar can switch to your Work space, the way Arc swaps spaces with its windows. Open **Preferences → Spaces → Switching spaces** to choose how this works.

- **When a linked browser/profile becomes active** offers **Ask to switch space**, **Switch automatically**, and **Do nothing**.
- **Open linked browser if it isn’t running** controls whether SupaSidebar launches the linked browser when you enter its space. It is off by default.

In **1.4.0 and later**, focusing a different window counts, not only switching apps. Moving from a Safari Work window to a Safari Personal window can switch spaces too.

### Which behavior you start with

| Your situation | Behavior |
|----------------|----------|
| You never chose a behavior | **Ask to switch space** |
| You link a profile before choosing a behavior | **Switch automatically** turns on |
| You already chose a behavior | Your choice is kept |

Before 1.4.0, the default was **Do nothing**. If you never changed it, SupaSidebar now asks instead.

### Ask to switch space

When you focus a window from another space's linked profile, a card in the sidebar names that space and offers **Move** or **Don't Move**. Check **Don't ask again** before you answer to make the choice permanent. **Move** then sets the behavior to **Switch automatically**, and **Don't Move** sets it to **Do nothing**.

### Switch automatically

The sidebar moves to the linked space on its own. An automatic switch moves only the sidebar, so your browser windows stay where they are. Switching spaces yourself, from the sidebar or a shortcut, works as before.

Every automatic switch shows a note in the sidebar, such as **Switched to Work**, that names the window that caused it.

- **Stop switching** - Goes back to the space you were in and sets the behavior to **Do nothing**
- **Don't show again** - Keeps switching automatically without the note
- **x button** - Hides this note only. The next automatic switch shows it again

In Compact Mode, the note appears as a **!** badge. Click it to read the note.

### Requirements

These switching controls require Live Tabs to be enabled. The connection itself and its default link destination do not require Live Tabs to be visible.

Switching to a linked space updates its tab list. Linking alone does not enable the legacy close-and-reopen flow.

## Close and reopen tabs per space

If you already use tab memory, its controls are under **Preferences → Spaces → Switching spaces → Advanced**. Enable **Close and reopen tabs on space change** only if you want that behavior.

This is a separate, legacy workflow. Closing can affect other profiles in the linked browser, and restoring a saved profile is best-effort. See [Close and reopen tabs (Legacy)](/features/linkOpenTabs) for its scope, tab locks, and restore behavior.

## Existing connections after updating

Your existing browser/profile links remain. If you had explicitly turned off automatic opening in linked profiles, that choice is preserved for those spaces. **Preferences → Spaces → My spaces** shows the space’s opening behavior. Choose **Open links in this browser or profile** if you want to enable it for that space.

New connections use the linked browser/profile as their default destination. This does not depend on whether Live Tabs is currently visible. If you later disable Air Traffic Control or its linked-space Open route, links follow the remaining opening settings.

## Permissions and missing tabs

The connection menu shows a permission warning when a required permission is missing. Use its action to open the relevant settings. You can also open **Preferences → Permission Status**.

Opening a URL and reading a browser's tab list can have different requirements. A successful open does not by itself verify tab access. See [Linked browser troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/profileSpaces) if the browser opens but its tabs do not appear.

## Unlink a browser or profile

Click the linked browser/profile next to the space name, then choose **Unlink**. The space returns to showing tabs from all enabled, supported browsers. Bookmarks you already added remain saved in the space.

To choose a different browser or profile, unlink the current one and use the link icon again.

## Find the right setting

| What you want to change | Where to go |
|-------------------------|-------------|
| This space's browser/profile or bookmarks | Link icon next to the space name |
| Manage connections across spaces | Preferences → Spaces → My spaces |
| Browser/profile switching behavior | Preferences → Spaces → Switching spaces |
| Live Tabs or Recents display | Preferences → Browser Activity |
| Which browsers can provide tabs or history | Preferences → Privacy & Browsers |
| Permission access | Preferences → Permission Status |
| Exceptions for particular links or sites | Preferred Browser, Open With, or Preferences → Air Traffic Control |

You can also [search settings](/howTo/searchSettings) instead of remembering these locations.
