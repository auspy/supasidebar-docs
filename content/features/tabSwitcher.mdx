# Tab Switcher

Tab Switcher lets you switch browser tabs the way `Cmd Tab` switches apps. Tap `Ctrl Tab` to go back to the tab you were on before, or hold `Ctrl` to pick from your 5 most recent tabs. It works across every browser you have open, like Arc's tab switcher.

<Callout type="info">
A Labs feature, new in SupaSidebar 1.6.0 and part of Pro. Turn on **Tab Switcher** in **Preferences → Labs** first. Without Pro, you can use it free for 30 days from the day you turn it on. See [Labs](/features/labs).
</Callout>

## How Tab Switcher works

- **Tap `Ctrl Tab`** - goes straight back to your last tab, with no window in the way. Tap again to flip between the two.
- **Hold `Ctrl` and press `Tab`** - shows your 5 most recent tabs. Keep holding `Ctrl` and press `Tab` to move down the list, or `Shift Tab` to move up. Let go of `Ctrl` to open the tab you picked.
- **Click a tab** in the switcher to open it right away.
- **Press `Ctrl Esc`** to close the switcher without switching.

The list starts with the tab you're on, then the tabs you used most recently, in any browser. Picking a tab from another browser brings that browser to the front.

Tab Switcher reads the same tab history as [Live Tabs](/features/liveTabs) and the sidebar's Back and Forward buttons. A tab appears once you've visited it while SupaSidebar is running, and only while it's still open.

## Only while a browser is in front

Tab Switcher only listens while a browser is the app in front. In Terminal, VS Code, Slack and every other app, `Ctrl Tab` keeps doing what it always did.

Inside a browser, `Ctrl Tab` opens Tab Switcher instead of the browser's next tab, like it does in Arc. Browsers you blocked in [Privacy & Browsers](/features/privacyBlocklist) count as other apps, so they keep their own `Ctrl Tab`.

## Choosing a layout

Open **Preferences → Tab Switcher** and click a layout. Each one shows a small drawing of how it looks.

| Layout | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Cards** (default) | A picture and title for each tab, like Arc |
| **Pictures** | Just pictures, like `Cmd Tab`, with the title below |
| **List** | Titles in a list, with a big picture of the one you pick |

The pictures are each page's preview image, the one a site shows when you share its link. SupaSidebar keeps them in memory only and never saves them to disk.

## Changing the shortcut

In **Preferences → Tab Switcher → Shortcut**, click the shortcut and press a new one. The rest follows your new keys.

- **Moving up the list** - your shortcut plus `Shift`. A shortcut that already uses `Shift` has no move-up key.
- **Closing without switching** - your shortcut's modifier keys plus `Esc`.
- **No modifier keys** - a shortcut without `Ctrl`, `Cmd`, `Opt` or `Shift` can't be held, so it always acts like a tap.

## Tab Switcher keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl Tab` (tap) | Go back to your last tab |
| `Ctrl Tab` (hold `Ctrl`) | Show your 5 most recent tabs |
| `Tab` while holding `Ctrl` | Move down the list |
| `Ctrl Shift Tab` | Move up the list |
| Let go of `Ctrl` | Open the selected tab |
| `Ctrl Esc` | Close the switcher without switching |

See [all keyboard shortcuts](/features/shortcuts) for the complete list.

## When the free month ends

Without Pro, Tab Switcher turns off when its free month ends. `Ctrl Tab` goes back to your browser's own next-tab shortcut, and your layout and shortcut are kept for when you upgrade.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| `Ctrl Tab` goes to the browser's next tab | Turn on **Tab Switcher** in **Preferences → Labs**. If the card says **Free month ended**, upgrade to Pro to keep using it |
| A tab is missing from the list | Tab Switcher lists only open tabs you've visited since SupaSidebar started. Visit the tab once and it appears |
| Nothing happens in one browser | Check that the browser isn't blocked in **Preferences → Privacy & Browsers** and that [Live Tabs](/features/liveTabs) shows its tabs |
