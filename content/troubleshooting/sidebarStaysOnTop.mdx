# Sidebar stays on top when you switch apps

## Problem

You switch from your browser to another app, like a text editor. The browser goes behind it as expected, but the sidebar keeps floating on top and covers part of the app.

## Cause

What the sidebar does when you leave the browser depends on your [Browser Integration](/features/browserIntegration) mode.

| Mode | When you switch to a non-browser app |
|------|--------------------------------------|
| **Smart Attach** (default) | Drops behind the app, comes back to the front with your browser |
| **Independent** | Stays on top of every app |

In either mode, **When Leaving Browser** set to **Hide** hides the sidebar instead. That setting only shows up while **Auto Show on Browser Focus** is on.

So the sidebar stays on top when it uses **Independent** and isn't set to hide when you leave the browser.

## Solution

Pick the fix that matches how you work.

### Let other apps cover the sidebar

1. Open **Preferences → Sidebar → Browser Integration**.
2. Set **Sidebar Browser Integration** to **Smart Attach**.
3. Switch from your browser to another app. The sidebar drops behind it.

The sidebar comes back to the front when you return to your browser. **Smart Attach** also places the sidebar next to your browser window. See [Smart Attach](/features/browserIntegration#smart-attach).

### Hide the sidebar when you leave the browser

1. Open **Preferences → Sidebar → Show and Hide**.
2. Under **Auto-Visibility**, turn on **Auto Show on Browser Focus**.
3. Set **When Leaving Browser** to **Hide**.

The sidebar now opens with your browser and hides when you switch to another app. This works with both **Smart Attach** and **Independent**.

## Still on top?

| Issue | Why | Fix |
|-------|-----|-----|
| It stays open in Finder | **Keep Showing in Finder** is on by default | Turn off **Keep Showing in Finder** under **When Leaving Browser** |
| You opened it with a shortcut outside a browser (**Independent**) | It only hides after a browser has owned it | Close it with `Cmd Shift Space` |
