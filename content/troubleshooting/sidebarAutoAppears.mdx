# Sidebar appears automatically when you open a browser

## Problem

The sidebar shows up on screen by itself. Every time you focus or open a browser window, the SupaSidebar panel slides in without you pressing a shortcut or clicking anything.

## Cause

This is **Auto-show with Browser** doing its job. When the setting is on, the sidebar automatically appears whenever a browser becomes the active app, so your links and tabs are ready the moment you start browsing.

If you would rather open the sidebar only when you ask for it (a keyboard shortcut, the menu bar icon, or a mouse trigger), turn this setting off.

## Solution

You can turn it off from the sidebar menu or from Preferences.

### From the sidebar menu

1. Open the sidebar and click the **...** button in the top toolbar.
2. Uncheck **Auto-show with Browser**.

![The sidebar toolbar menu with Auto-show with Browser highlighted](/assets/auto-show-with-browser-menu.png)

### From Preferences

1. Open **Preferences → Sidebar**.
2. Turn off **Auto Show on Browser Focus**.

Once it is off, the sidebar stays hidden until you open it yourself. Nothing else about your links, spaces, or [Smart Attach](/features/browserIntegration/attach) layout changes.

## Still want auto-show, just less often?

If you like the sidebar appearing with your browser but want it to get out of the way afterward, leave **Auto-show with Browser** on and set **When Leaving Browser** to **Hide**. The sidebar then appears with the browser and hides again the moment you switch to another app.
